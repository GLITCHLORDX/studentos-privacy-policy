StudentOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

StudentOS is a student-focused Android application designed to bring study, productivity, focus, safety, and other student tools together in one place.

1. Information We Collect

StudentOS may process information that you provide while using the application. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Student profile and preference information.
  • Subjects, notes, flashcards, quizzes, exams, courses, and study information.
  • Tasks, focus sessions, and study sessions.
  • Gamification information such as XP, streaks, and achievements.
  • Emergency contact information that you choose to save.
  • AI conversations and supported attachments that you submit to the AI features.
  • Information required to synchronize supported app data across devices.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

StudentOS stores certain information locally on your Android device so that features can work efficiently and, where supported, while offline.

Local storage may include application settings, locally cached content, student data, and other information necessary for the application's operation.

3. Cloud Synchronization

StudentOS provides account-based cloud synchronization for supported user data. When you use synchronization features, applicable data may be transmitted to and stored using Supabase services.

Cloud synchronization is intended to allow supported StudentOS data to be restored when you sign in to the same account on another or newly installed device.

4. Authentication

StudentOS uses account authentication to associate synchronized data with your account. Authentication information is processed by the authentication services used by the application.

5. StudentOS AI

StudentOS includes AI-powered features that can help with study-related interactions.

When you submit a prompt, supported document, image, or other attachment to an AI feature, the submitted content may be transmitted to the AI service configured by StudentOS in order to generate a response.

AI conversations may also be stored as part of your StudentOS data so that your conversation history and supported synchronized data can be restored.

6. Documents and Images

StudentOS may allow you to select documents or images for supported features, including AI interactions.

Files are processed only when required by the feature you choose to use. You should avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information unless you are comfortable with the processing required by that feature.

7. Emergency and Safety Features

StudentOS includes optional emergency and safety functionality.

If you choose to use SOS or related emergency functionality, the application may use permissions such as calling, SMS messaging, and location access to perform the emergency action you requested.

Location information used by emergency functionality is intended to support the emergency action initiated by you. These permissions are not intended to provide continuous location tracking when the emergency feature is not being used.

Emergency contacts are contacts that you choose to configure within the application.

8. Permissions

StudentOS may request Android permissions that are necessary for particular features. Depending on the features you use, these can include permissions related to notifications, storage or media access, location, phone calls, and SMS messaging.

You can manage Android permissions through your device's system settings. Some StudentOS features may not work correctly if required permissions are denied.

9. Data Security

StudentOS uses local Android storage and authenticated cloud services to protect application data. Access to synchronized user data is restricted according to the application's account and authorization mechanisms.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Deletion

StudentOS provides account and application data-management functionality. Signing out removes the applicable user data from the local device according to the application's local data-wipe behavior.

If you want to request deletion of account-associated cloud data or have a privacy-related question, contact the developer using the contact method provided below.

11. Third-Party Services

StudentOS may use third-party services to provide authentication, cloud synchronization, and AI functionality.

These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functionality and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

12. Children's Privacy

StudentOS is designed as an educational and productivity application for students. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information beyond what is necessary for the application's functionality.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to StudentOS improperly, please contact the developer so that the matter can be reviewed.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to StudentOS, its services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data-deletion requests, or other concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the StudentOS developer through the official StudentOS project repository.
